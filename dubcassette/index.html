A tape machine, not a tape preset

Every echo runs through a real tape path: the transport wobbles, the record head saturates, the playback head rolls off the top and bumps the low end, and the tape hisses. Change the tape and all of it changes.

Four speeds, four machines

Hold to brake

Hold the key and the transport winds down to silence. Let go and it spins back up into the live signal. Set the brake from 0.1 to 3 seconds, and automate it.

Questions

How do I install it?
  1. Double-click Dubcassette-Installer.pkg from your download and follow the steps. It installs the AU, VST3 and Standalone app (untick any you don't want).
  2. Open your DAW. In Logic, click an Audio FX slot and choose Audio Units → Sounding Sonics → Dubcassette.
How many computers can I use it on?

Two. Your Gumroad receipt includes a license key; paste it into Dubcassette the first time you open it (this needs internet once). If you replace a computer, reply to your receipt email and we'll move the license for you.

Does varispeed change my song's tempo?

In the plugin, varispeed changes pitch and feel in real time while your session keeps its tempo, because a plugin can't play audio that hasn't arrived yet. Slowing down and the tape stop work fully. Use it on a bus, an instrument or the echoes for pitch drops, rises and tape-stop effects.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Dubcassette is Mac-only for now. The browser version on this page runs on any computer.

Which DAWs does it run in?

Any Mac host that loads Audio Unit or VST3 effects, including Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig and Reaper. The Standalone app runs without a DAW.
