Questions

How do I install it?
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy Machmaster.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and Machmaster.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  3. Restart your DAW. In Logic, add it to your Stereo Out from Audio FX → Audio Units → Sounding Sonics → Machmaster.
Where does it go in my session?

On your master or stereo out, after your mix bus processing. You can also use it on a bounced stereo mix in its own project.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Machmaster is Mac-only for now. The browser version on this page runs on any computer.

Which DAWs does it run in?

Any Mac host that loads Audio Unit or VST3 effects, including Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig and Reaper. The Standalone app runs without a DAW.
