Products

Supersonic $45

An FM and phase-distortion synth that draws every note you play as a star, with a Warp pad you drag to reshape the sound.

  • FM and phase-distortion engines
  • Warp pad for hands-on sound shaping
  • Visual modulation: watch your sound move

Machmaster $90

A mastering suite that listens to your mix and sets itself up, taking a finished mix to release-ready loudness.

  • EQ, exciter, multiband dynamics, stereo imager and true-peak maximizer
  • Listen mode sets the chain up from your mix
  • Built-in loudness (LUFS) meters

About Sounding Sonics

Ready to paste into an article, video description or show notes.

Short

Sounding Sonics makes synths and mastering tools for Mac, priced as one-time purchases with no subscription. Every plugin can be tried free in the browser before you buy.

Long

Sounding Sonics is an independent plugin maker that builds instruments and mastering tools for Mac, made by a producer and tested in real sessions first. Its plugins run as AU, VST3 and Standalone apps in Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig and Reaper, and each one can be played free in a web browser before buying.

The first two releases, launching October 28, 2026, are Supersonic ($45), an FM and phase-distortion synth that draws every note you play as a star, and Machmaster ($90), a mastering suite that listens to a mix and sets itself up. Both are one-time purchases with no subscription. More at soundingsonics.com.
