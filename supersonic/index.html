Questions

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Supersonic is Mac-only for now. You can still play the browser demo on any computer.

How do I install it?
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Copy Supersonic.component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and Supersonic.vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
  3. Restart your DAW and load Supersonic on an instrument track (in Logic: AU Instruments → Sounding Sonics → Supersonic).
How many computers can I use it on?

Two. Your Gumroad receipt includes a license key; paste it into Supersonic the first time you open it (this needs internet once). If you replace a computer, reply to your receipt email and we'll move the license for you.

Which DAWs does it run in?

Any Mac host that loads Audio Units or VST3 instruments, including Logic Pro, GarageBand, Ableton Live, FL Studio, Bitwig and Reaper. The Standalone app runs without a DAW.
