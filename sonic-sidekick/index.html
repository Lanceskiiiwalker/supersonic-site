Everything it does

Screenshot and ask

⌃⌥S anywhere: the arrange window, the piano roll, a plugin. It reads track names, notes and knob values, and answers about what you're actually looking at.

Talk to it

Hands on your keys? Press ⌃⌥V and say it: "darker chords", "more bounce in the hats". It uses Apple's built-in speech recognition.

MIDI straight into Logic

Drum grooves, chord progressions, basslines and melodies in your tempo and key. Play them into the selected track, record in one click, or drag the file in.

Mix check

Drop a bounce and it measures loudness (LUFS), true peak, dynamics, tonal balance, stereo width and low-end mono, then tells you what to fix and by how much.

Turns Machmaster's knobs

With Machmaster 1.5 or later open, ask "master this for streaming" and Sidekick sets EQ, dynamics and the limiter, reads the meters, and adjusts again. Every change has Undo.

Knows your tools

Patch recipes for Alchemy, Retro Synth, ES2, Sculpture and Drum Machine Designer, plus Supersonic and Machmaster settings, and arrangement plans with bar counts.

Questions

How do I install it?
  1. Unzip SonicSidekick-mac.zip from your download and drag Sonic Sidekick into Applications.
  2. Open it. A waveform icon appears in your menu bar, and the panel asks for the license key from your Gumroad receipt.
  3. Paste your Claude API key in Settings, or choose the free mode. Then press ⌃⌥S over Logic and ask something.
What's a Claude API key and how do I get one?

It's how the app pays Anthropic for each answer. Sign in at console.anthropic.com, add a few dollars of credit under Billing, then create a key under API Keys and paste it into Sonic Sidekick's Settings. A Claude.ai chat subscription doesn't include API use; it's billed separately.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay $50 once for the app. If you use Claude for answers, Anthropic bills your own account for what you use (usually a cent or a few cents per question). Free mode costs nothing per question.

What gets sent, and where?

Only what you ask with: your question and any screenshot or mix measurements, sent to Anthropic when you press send. Mix checks measure your bounce on your Mac; the audio file itself is never uploaded. In free mode nothing leaves your Mac. The Machmaster link stays on your Mac too.

Does it change my session by itself?

Only when you ask. It never clicks around in Logic. It can play MIDI into the track you've selected when you press Play, and turn Machmaster's knobs when you ask it to master or fix something, with an Undo button for every change.

Do I need Machmaster?

No. Everything except Machmaster control works on its own. If you have Machmaster, update to 1.5 or later and Sidekick can drive it.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Two. If you replace a Mac, reply to your Gumroad receipt email and we'll free up a spot.

Does it work on Windows?

Not yet. Sonic Sidekick is Mac-only for now.
